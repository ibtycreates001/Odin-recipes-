Jollof Rice

Description

A nigerian rice cooked in rich, spiced tomato and pepper broth

Ingredients

  • rice
  • tomatoes
  • salt
  • seasonings
  • spices
  • pepper
  • onions
  • protein
  • butter

Steps

  1. Prep you proteins with salt, seasonings, spice and onions then fry
  2. Soak your rice in hot water
  3. Fry your onions, pepper and tomato mixed,add seasonings,spice,salt and your protein broth
  4. Add your rice after washing it and allow to cook
  5. When almost done add some slices of onion and add butter then garnish with your protein
