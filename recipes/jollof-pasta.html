Pasta

Description

A delicious, smoky, and spicy Nigerian fusion dish where pasta cooks directly in or gets tossed with a rich, pepper-tomato Jollof stew base

Ingredients

  • pasta
  • tomatoes
  • salt
  • seasonings
  • spices
  • pepper
  • onions
  • protein
  • butter

Steps

  1. prep you proteins with salt, seasonings, spice and onions then fry
  2. parboil your pasta
  3. fry your onions, pepper and tomato mixed,add seasonings,spice,salt and your protein broth
  4. add your parboiled pasta and allow to cook
  5. when it is done, garnish with your protein
